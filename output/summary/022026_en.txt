Autonomous Field Navigation of Robot (022026) is a 2-month internship.
Develop an autonomous navigation stack for a small field robot, using LiDAR and positioning sensors for obstacle detection and safe movement.
The main goals are to enable autonomous field navigation, detect obstacles using LiDAR data, and fuse localization and perception signals for safer motion.
Typical work includes integrate LiDAR, GPS, and Pixhawk interfaces, build localization and navigation modules, and detect obstacles from LiDAR observations.
The role is best suited for candidates with Python programming, Robotics fundamentals, and ROS basics.
Expected deliverables include Autonomous navigation prototype with LiDAR-based obstacle detection and Field validation results.
