Autonomous Field Navigation with Radar (022026) is a 2-month internship. Develop an autonomous navigation stack for a small field robot with obstacle detection supported by radar and positioning sensors. The main goals are to enable autonomous field navigation, detect obstacles using radar data, and fuse localization and perception signals for safer motion. Typical work includes integrate lidar GPS pixhawk and radar interfaces, build localization and navigation modules, and detect obstacles from radar observations. The role is best suited for candidates with Python programming, Robotics fundamentals, and ROS basics. Expected deliverables include Autonomous navigation prototype with radar-based obstacle detection and Field validation results. Payment: Available in Bizquick. Supervisor: Kaleb Granados.
