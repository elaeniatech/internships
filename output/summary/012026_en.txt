Vegetation and Obstacle Perception (012026) is a 2-month internship. Develop a perception pipeline to estimate grass height detect invasive weeds and identify field obstacles for a small ground robot. The main goals are to estimate grass height in field conditions, detect invasive weeds in camera imagery, and identify poles and other obstacles around the robot. Typical work includes build image and ranging acquisition modules, process camera and laser data to estimate grass height, and detect invasive weeds and mark detections. The role is best suited for candidates with Python programming, Computer vision fundamentals, and ROS basics. Expected deliverables include Perception prototype for grass height weeds and obstacle detection and Test results from field trials. Payment: Available in Bizquick. Supervisor: Kaleb Granados.
